INTRODUCTION

What are Alkaloids?

🌿

Definition

Alkaloids are naturally occurring organic compounds that generally contain nitrogen atoms and often show significant physiological effects on humans and other organisms.

🔬

Structure

Many alkaloids contain complex ring structures with nitrogen atoms. Their structures are responsible for their diverse chemical and biological properties.

💡

Importance

Alkaloids occur in many plants and have been studied for their medicinal, physiological and chemical properties.

Methoxy Group

A methoxy group is a functional group consisting of a methyl group bonded to an oxygen atom. It is represented as –OCH₃.
